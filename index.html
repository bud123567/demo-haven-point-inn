Field note 01 · The inn

A small stay with more than one point of view.

Step outside for marinas and Rock Hall’s shops. Stay in for an oven-to-table breakfast, original art, and a garden made to be looked over slowly.

At the inn 03 · Gallery & garden

Breakfast is served where the art lives.

The gallery café, gift shop, and European-style potager garden give the inn its own daily rhythm.

03.1

Oven to table

Complimentary gourmet breakfast comes with fresh-ground coffee and may draw on farm-fresh eggs, fruit, imported cheeses, San Marzano tomatoes, and herbs from the garden.

Vegan and gluten-free dishes are available.

03.2

A working view

The potager garden is part view, part pantry. Guests can also borrow cruising bicycles for getting around town; mountain and hybrid rentals are available.

Around Rock Hall 04 · On foot, bike, or water

Walk out into Rock Hall.

The inn’s central marina-district address puts restaurants, shops, and Ferry Park Beach within a short walk.

Go by bicycle
Flat terrain, complimentary cruising bikes for guests, and mountain or hybrid rentals.
Follow the shoreline
Swimming and sunsets at Ferry Park Beach, plus creeks and waterways around town.
Head farther out
Eastern Neck National Wildlife Refuge is a few miles away. Sea-kayak, fishing-charter, and sailing options are also available in the area.
